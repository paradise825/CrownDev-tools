About Crown Dev

Crown Dev builds simple digital tools that help people get everyday tasks done quickly, without sign-ups, without ads, and without giving away their files.

What we do

We create small, practical tools for everyday work. Right now, our site includes an image resizer and a CV builder. Both run directly in your browser, so your files stay on your own device.

Why we built it

We believe useful tools should be simple, fast, and private. Most people only need a small helper for one task, and they should not need an account or a complicated setup to use it.
